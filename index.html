Features

Everything your sales team needs

No bloat, no complex setup. Just the tools that help you capture more leads and close more deals.

Lead Management

Capture, track, and nurture leads through every stage of your pipeline. Never let a prospect slip through the cracks.

Email Campaigns

Send personalised emails directly from your CRM. Track opens, bounces, and replies — all in one place.

Custom Objects

Build your own data structures. Define fields, layouts, and relationships that match exactly how your business works.

Comments & Notes

Keep your team in sync with threaded comments, @mentions, and a full activity timeline on every record.

File Attachments

Attach contracts, proposals, and documents directly to records. Everything your team needs — right where they need it.

Role-Based Access

Control exactly who can view, create, edit, or delete data. Fine-grained permissions for every team member.
